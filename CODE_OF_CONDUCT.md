# Code of Conduct

VideoToolbox Remote should be a practical, respectful place to discuss FFmpeg, VideoToolbox, media pipelines, and related tooling.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on facts, tradeoffs, and reproducible behavior.
- Assume contributors are working in good faith unless there is clear evidence otherwise.
- Provide enough context for others to reproduce, review, or understand your point.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained off-topic disruption.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Spam or intentionally misleading reports.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may block users when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

To report a conduct issue, contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile linked from the repository owner account.
